# Ariba Jahan

I work where a shift in technology, customer behavior, or the market creates a question an organization needs to answer: what does this mean for the people we serve, what should we build or change, and how do we make it work in practice?

I build products, experiences, and services that answer those questions, from early research and strategy through the systems, teams, and operating decisions that make the work real.

Over the past fifteen years, I have built products, customer experiences, service offerings, go-to-market strategies, teams, and innovation practices across public health, enterprise technology, consumer products, nonprofits, and agencies.

My recent work has focused on designing products and experiences that are genuinely useful, and helping organizations put AI to work in ways that strengthen their expertise, change how teams operate, and become part of the way the organization works beyond a pilot.

My background in bioengineering research and medical training shapes how I approach the work: start with evidence, make the problem concrete, build something people can use, and learn from what happens next.

## What I build

- **Products, experiences, and tools:** research, product strategy, concepts, prototypes, roadmaps, and delivery with product, design, data, and engineering partners.
- **Practices and capabilities:** innovation teams, internal consultancies, AI workflows, training, and operating models that help organizations use new technology in daily work.
- **Research and public work:** evidence reviews, writing, and frameworks about how AI changes human judgment, behavior, and organizational practice.

- **AI products, adoption, and human judgment:** turning AI capability into useful products and workflows while designing for the people, decisions, and operating practices around it.
- **[Cognitive Endurance](https://www.unmissables.xyz/p/cognitive-endurance-2):** a two-part framework for developing stronger human-AI loops: using AI to extend intelligence while strengthening discernment, creativity, critical thinking, and independent judgment. ## How I build

I use Claude Design, Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Claude Science, Perplexity Computer, and other AI tools as a hands-on building practice: testing product ideas, designing workflows, developing visual systems, creating tools that help people make better decisions, and investigating questions that need more than a quick answer.

I’m building Ariba Jahan OS, an agentic system I use to run my work across research, planning, writing, product development, and project management. For example, research agents run on a weekly schedule against named sources and produce dated reports. A separate agent audits those reports against the rules the research agents are supposed to follow, fixes what it can in the files, and flags what it can’t. A drift-detection agent finds broken references across the system and repairs the ones where there’s only one correct answer. I publish selected parts of the system in [Claude Skills](https://github.com/aribajahan/claude-skills).

[AI and Cognition](https://github.com/aribajahan/ai-cognition) is a structured evidence review of what current research has measured about AI and adult cognition: memory, learning and skill, judgment, attention, and self-assessment. I built it with Claude Science and Perplexity Computer, then examined what each study measured and how far its findings could go. The public repository includes the synthesis, methods, evidence tables, field notes, and an [interactive question map](https://aribajahan.github.io/ai-cognition/question-map/). The [Claude Science skills](https://github.com/aribajahan/claude-science-skills) grew out of that research and cover source integrity, extraction, full-text retrieval, and construct audits.

I built [aribajahan.com](https://github.com/aribajahan/ariba-jahan-site), its [brand book](https://github.com/aribajahan/ariba-site-brand-book), and the CMS behind it with Claude Code, Claude Design, and Codex. I use Granola, Gemini, and Google Workspace alongside them to research, write, organize, and publish the work.
