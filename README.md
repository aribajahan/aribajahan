# Ariba Jahan

I work where a shift in technology, customer behavior, or the market creates a question an organization needs to answer: what does this mean for the people we serve, what should we build or change, and how do we make it work in practice?

I build products, experiences, and services that answer those questions, from early research and strategy through the systems, teams, and operating decisions that make the work real.

Over the past fifteen years, I have built products, customer experiences, service offerings, go-to-market strategies, teams, and innovation practices across public health, enterprise technology, consumer products, nonprofits, and agencies.

My recent work has focused on designing products and experiences that are genuinely useful, and helping organizations put AI to work in ways that strengthen their expertise, change how teams operate, and become part of the way the organization works beyond a pilot.

My background in bioengineering research and medical training shapes how I approach the work: start with evidence, make the problem concrete, build something people can use, and learn from what happens next.

## What I build

- **Products, experiences, and tools** : research, product strategy, concepts, prototypes, roadmaps, and delivery with product, design, data, and engineering partners.
- **Practices and capabilities** : innovation teams, internal consultancies, AI workflows, training, and operating models that help organizations use new technology in daily work.
- **Research and public work** : evidence reviews, writing, and frameworks about how AI changes human judgment, behavior, and organizational practice.

## Areas of practice

- **Experience-Led Growth** : helping organizations create products, services, and customer experiences that earn a meaningful place in people’s lives.
- **AI products, adoption, and human judgment** : turning AI capability into useful products and workflows while designing for the people, decisions, and operating practices around it.
- **Cognitive Endurance** : a two-part framework for building a stronger human-AI practice. I develop this work through [Unmissables](https://unmissables.xyz), my newsletter and podcast, and through the [AI and cognition research project](https://github.com/aribajahan/ai-cognition).

## How I build

I use Claude Design, Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Claude Science, Perplexity Computer, and other AI tools as a hands-on building practice: testing product ideas, designing workflows, developing visual systems, creating tools that help people make better decisions, and investigating questions that need more than a quick answer.

I’m building Ariba Jahan OS, the AI-supported system I use to run my work across research, planning, writing, product development, and project management. It helps me carry context across projects, turn repeated work into reusable workflows, and move from an idea to something working. I publish selected parts of that system in [Claude Skills](https://github.com/aribajahan/claude-skills).

[AI and Cognition](https://github.com/aribajahan/ai-cognition) is an ongoing research project on how AI use affects adult cognition, built through literature-review workflows in Claude Science and Perplexity Computer. The [Claude Science skills](https://github.com/aribajahan/claude-science-skills) grew out of that research and cover source integrity, extraction, full-text retrieval, and construct audits.

I built [aribajahan.com](https://github.com/aribajahan/ariba-jahan-site), its [brand system](https://github.com/aribajahan/ariba-design-system), and the CMS behind it with Claude Code, Claude Design, and Codex. I use Granola, Gemini, and Google Workspace alongside them to research, write, organize, and publish the work.

## Other public projects

- **[Stay Ready](https://github.com/aribajahan/stay-ready)** : a tool for people preparing for encounters with ICE or law enforcement, with emergency contacts, documents, and rights information in six languages.
- **[Plan Your Birth Story](https://github.com/aribajahan/plan-your-birth-story)** : an AI-assisted birth-planning prototype that informed my current work on Asha.
- **[Little Playroom](https://github.com/aribajahan/toddler-games-app)** : calm, creative games for children ages 4–8, built for my son.
- **[Library Finder](https://github.com/aribajahan/library-finder)** : a browser extension that checks whether a book is available through a public library before you buy it.
